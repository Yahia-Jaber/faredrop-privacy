FareDrop Support

For FareDrop on Android and iPhone

Contact

Email appsupport77@gmail.com. We read every message and usually answer within a few days.

How alerts work

  • Fares are checked on our server, so the app does not need to be open.
  • You get a notification when a route you track drops in price, and when it reaches the target price you set.
  • Fare data can be delayed. An alert is a signal to check; the price on the booking page is the one that counts.

No alerts arriving

  • Check that notifications are allowed for FareDrop in your phone's settings.
  • Open the app once after reinstalling it or setting up a new phone, so it can register for alerts again.

FareDrop Pro

  • Cancel or manage on iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions.
  • Cancel or manage on Android: Google Play, your profile picture, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions.
  • Restore Pro after reinstalling or on a new phone: open Account, tap FareDrop Pro, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple or Google account you bought it with.
  • Refunds are handled by the store that billed you: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google Play through your order history.

Your data

FareDrop has no accounts. To remove your data, delete your routes in the app. If you have uninstalled the app and want leftover routes removed, email us your approximate install date and the routes you tracked.
