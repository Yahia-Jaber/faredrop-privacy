Privacy Policy for FareDrop

Effective date: November 5, 2026

FareDrop is a flight price tracker for Android and iPhone. You choose routes and a target price, and FareDrop watches the fares and alerts you when a price drops. It is distributed through Google Play (package name io.github.yahia_jaber.faredrop) and the Apple App Store.

Summary

  • FareDrop has no accounts. You never give us your name or email address to use the app.
  • The routes you watch and your target prices are stored on our server so that we can check fares and send you alerts.
  • Your device is identified to our server only by a random push notification token, or by a random install identifier when notifications are off.
  • We do not sell your information and we do not show third party ads.

Information we collect

  • Watched routes: the origin and destination airports, your target price and when you added the route. These are sent to our server, because fare checks run there even while the app is closed.
  • Push notification token or install identifier: a random token issued for your app install, used to deliver price alerts and to find your routes when you open the app. When notifications are not allowed, a random identifier made by the app is used instead. Neither contains your name, phone number or email address.
  • Device type and app version, as part of ordinary network requests and push delivery.
  • ZIP code, optional: during setup you may type a ZIP code so the app can suggest nearby airports. The ZIP is used on your device for that suggestion and is not required.

FareDrop does not access your contacts, photos, precise location or calendar.

How your information is used

To check fares on the routes you watch, to send you price alerts, to show your routes when you reopen the app, and to recognise your subscription. Nothing else.

Where it is processed

Our server runs on Railway in the United States. Fare data comes from third party flight price sources; we send them the route and dates to search, never anything that identifies you. Push notifications are delivered through Expo's push service and then through Google's notification service on Android or Apple's Push Notification service on iPhone.

Subscriptions and payments

FareDrop offers an optional Pro subscription. Purchases are made through Google Play on Android or the Apple App Store on iPhone, and are billed by Google or by Apple under their own terms. We never see or store your card or bank details.

To recognise your subscription across devices and app installs, we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) as our subscription processor. RevenueCat receives a random identifier for your app install, your purchase records and subscription status from Google Play or the App Store, your device type and your app version. It does not receive your name, your email address or your payment details. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

You can cancel a subscription at any time: on Android from the Subscriptions page in Google Play, on iPhone in Settings, under your name, Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; Pro stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. A free trial becomes a paid subscription when it ends unless you cancel before then. Purchase records are kept for as long as your subscription is active and afterwards only as long as accounting and tax rules require.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with advertisers or data brokers. Service providers named above (Railway, Expo, Google, Apple, RevenueCat and the fare sources) process data only to run the service.

Retention and deletion

Watched routes stay on our server until you remove them in the app. Uninstalling the app leaves behind routes tied to a push token that no longer works; email us with your approximate install date and routes and we will remove them. Push tokens that stop working are removed.

Security

Traffic between the app and our server is encrypted. No system can guarantee absolute security, which is one reason FareDrop stores as little about you as it does.

Children's privacy

FareDrop is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes are published on this page with a new effective date.
